How the fold works

  • Every piece is a flat, rigid plate. Every seam is a hinge about the seam line.
  • Edges that touch in 2D are seams on their own. Explicit seams pull separate pieces and assemblies together.
  • Fabric would rather lie flat: each hinge bends only as far as closing the seams needs. The fold starts by curling the design away from you, so the right side ends up outside.
  • Seams that still don't close once the fold settles are listed above and drawn red.

Questions this raises

  • Stuffing: does the firmness range cover how quilters stuff (loose to hard)? Should the fabric's stretch be a setting?
  • Pieces may pass through each other on the way (there's no collision yet). Does that matter once the fold has settled?
  • The star can still flex (it isn't rigid). Should the quilter be able to pull a piece by hand to see that?
  • Should the 2D design show which side each piece folds to (mountain or valley)?
