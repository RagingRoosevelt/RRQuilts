A rosette is a group of English paper piecing shapes sewn edge to edge, such as a hexagon flower or a six-diamond star. It has no size of its own. An EPP quilt sets its 1U length, and a block quilt sets it from the block space it fills (see [English paper piecing](/help/guides/epp-quilts#units)). Open a rosette from the project's **EPP** tab, or choose **New rosette** there. The rosette editor has **Details**, **Design**, **Print** and **3D Print** tabs. Library rosettes open read-only.

## Place shapes

In the **Design** tab, the sidebar's **Shapes** tab picks what to place. **Shapes and rosettes** lists this project's, then the libraries', and the **Scale** slider sets the size (1 to 4). A rosette placed in a rosette becomes its pieces and doesn't stay linked.

**Placement** works as in an EPP quilt. With **Free** you place and drag pieces, with **Grow** you tap a free edge to attach the picked shape, and with **Grid** pieces sit on a grid (**Drag grid** and **Sync grid to selected size** line it up). A dragged piece snaps when one of its edges comes within about half an edge of a neighbor's, turning up to 30° to fit, and meeting edges show green. Pieces may overlap.

The tools on the left are **Select**, **Place**, **Turn**, **Mirror left to right**, **Mirror up and down**, **Delete**, **Close space**, **Paint** and **Duplicate**. They work as in an EPP quilt's Design tab (see [English paper piecing](/help/guides/epp-quilts#the-design-tab)). **Close space** fills a gap with a shape. When the gap matches one of your shapes or a library's it uses that one. Otherwise it makes a shape that belongs to the rosette.

A shape made in the rosette that can't be used yet is outlined in red, and the banner above says why. One with edges that aren't whole units is outlined in orange. Those edges snap to edges of the same length, and to edges double, half or a quarter as long.

## Color a rosette

Colors work as in a block. The sidebar's **Colors** tab (the **Paint** tool) shows the rosette's **Colorings**, each as a picture of the rosette in its colors, the way a quilt shows its colorways. Tap one to work on it, **New coloring** adds one, and **Copy** copies the one shown. The pencil under a coloring renames it and the bin deletes it. Pick a color or fabric under **Colors and fabrics** and tap pieces to paint them in that coloring.

**New coloring from palette** makes a coloring from the one shown, as a quilt's colorways do (see [Designing a quilt](/help/guides/designing-a-quilt)). Pick a palette and each color becomes its nearest there, and you can change any of them. Then pick a **Mode**:

- **Palette** keeps following the coloring it came from. Repaint that one and the new coloring changes too, with your picks.
- **Auto map** also follows the palette. Each color stays its nearest in the palette as either changes, except the ones you pick by hand.
- **Re-color** starts with the new colors, and then you paint it like any coloring.

A coloring that follows another shows its mode under the buttons, with what it **Follows** and a row for each color. Pick a different coloring under **Follows** and your picks stay. Switch the mode to **Re-color** to paint it yourself. A painted coloring can follow another too: pick **Palette** or **Auto map** (its painting goes, and Undo brings it back). Deleting a coloring leaves the others looking as they did.

A rosette with several colorings shows how many on its card in the shape picker. Tap the number to choose the coloring before you place it. In an EPP quilt, painting a piece of a placed rosette changes it in that quilt only.

## Rosettes in block quilts

A rosette whose outline is exactly a hexagon, a triangle, a 60° diamond or a square can fill a block space of that shape. In a block quilt's **Design** tab, the block tray's **Rosettes** switch lists the rosettes that fit the quilt's spaces. Set one like a block. It takes the space's size, and it prints as paper and fabric templates, not as rotary cut pieces.

## Print a rosette

The **Print** tab can print a **Rosette sheet** (a picture of the rosette), **Paper templates (finished size)** and fabric templates, in the coloring picked in the Design tab. Set the **1U length** and **How many rosettes**, and the templates count each shape for that many. Fabric templates use the project's EPP seam allowance. The **3D Print** tab makes cutting templates to print on a 3D printer. See [Printing](/help/guides/printing).

## Share a rosette

**Export (.rrqepp)** in a rosette's card menu saves it as a file, with the shapes and colors it uses. **Import** in the EPP tab brings one in. **Add to My library** keeps it for every project (see [Libraries](/help/guides/libraries)).
