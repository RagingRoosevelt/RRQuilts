A 3D EPP design is a flat layout of pieces whose edges are sewn into an object, such as a ball, a die or a stuffed star. It is listed with the project's quilts and in its **EPP** tab, with a **3D** badge.

## Start a 3D design

1. Open the project's **EPP** tab and click **New 3D design**. Or start a quilt, choose **EPP** and turn on **3D EPP**.
2. In the **Design** tab, place shapes and rosettes as in any EPP quilt. Pieces that touch are sewn along the edges they share.
3. Set the **1U length** in the **Layout** tab. A 3D design has no edge, borders or binding.

An EPP quilt's **Layout** tab has the same **3D EPP** switch. Turning it on removes the quilt's edge and borders (Undo brings them back). Turning it off keeps the seams for when it is 3D again.

## Sew edges that don't touch

Most objects can't lie flat in one piece, so some edges only meet once the design is folded. The **Seam** tool sews them.

1. Choose **Seam** in the Design tab's toolbar.
2. Tap a free edge, then an edge of the same length on another piece. Both edges take the seam's color and number.
3. Tap a numbered edge to unpick its seam.

The layout can hold several separate groups of pieces joined only by seams. The 3D assembly tab pulls them together.

## The 3D assembly tab

The tab shows the flat design above the folded object. **Settings** › **Interface** › **3D EPP** can put them side by side instead. In the flat design, tap edges to sew or unpick seams as with the Seam tool. Seams the fold can't close are red there.

The design folds the way fabric with stiff papers inside does. Each piece stays flat and every seam bends like a hinge. Pieces fold no further than the seams need, so five diamonds sewn into a ring make a shallow star.

- Drag to turn the object. Use the mouse wheel or pinch to zoom.
- Click the object to spread its pieces apart and see the stitches across every seam. Click again to close it up.
- Once folded, the object turns so its front faces you and the design's up is up. An open shape faces the way its pieces face overall. A closed shape shows one piece in front, the piece nearest the middle of its largest group unless you double-click another. **Default front** goes back to that piece. The Design tab rings the front piece.
- **Fold again** starts the fold over.

The side panel says whether every seam closes. Seams that can't close are listed and drawn red, and the object shows the closest fold it found.

## Stuffing

**Stuffed design** is a setting of the design, saved with it. It is in the **Layout** tab and in the 3D assembly tab's side panel. A stuffed design fills once it has folded: the pieces become fabric, the stuffing pushes them out, and the shape rounds the way fabric that hardly stretches does.

Only a closed shape can be stuffed, and a shape is closed when every edge is sewn. The side panel says whether the design can be stuffed and, when it can't, why.

**Enable simulation view** only changes what the 3D assembly tab shows. It is on whenever the tab opens a stuffed design. Turn it off to see the flat pieces. Nothing is saved.

Some designs only close once stuffed. Two stars sewn back to back lie flat as papers, and a pincushion sewn one edge round can't close with flat pieces at all. For a stuffed design the side panel says the fabric closes those seams, with the simulation view on or off. For a closed design that isn't stuffed, it lists them and says stuffing might close them.

## Print

The **Print** tab gives paper and fabric templates as for any EPP quilt. The quilt picture shows each seam's two edges in its color with its number, so you can match them up while sewing.
