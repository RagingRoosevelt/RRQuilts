A shape is one English paper piecing template. Its edges are counted in units (see [English paper piecing](/help/guides/epp-quilts#units)). Open one from the project's **EPP** tab, or choose **New shape** there to start from a 1U square. The shape editor has **Details**, **Draw**, **Print** and **3D Print** tabs. Library shapes open read-only.

## Draw

The outline sits on a grid of units. The line under the picture gives the number of corners, or where the pointer is.

- **Vertex** moves a corner when you drag it. Tap an edge to add a corner there.
- **Edge** moves an edge, both corners with it. Tap an edge to mark it as not a whole unit, and tap it again to clear the mark.
- **Angle** changes a corner's angle. Drag the angle's label to the right to widen it or to the left to narrow it, one **Angle step** (15° to start) at a time. The edges keep their lengths.
- **Lock vertex** fixes a corner so no tool moves it. Tap it again to free it.
- **Delete** removes a corner you tap, or joins an edge's two corners into one.

**Vertex grid snapping** decides where corners land. **Free** puts them anywhere, **Square** on whole units, and **Triangle** on a grid of 60° triangles for hexagons, diamonds and triangles. **Undo** and **Redo** are at the top of the tools.

## Checks

The sidebar checks the shape as you draw. It **Closes**, it **Doesn't cross itself** and it is **Convex** (no corner points inward). A shape that fails one of these can't be used in a rosette or a quilt. **Every edge a whole unit or marked** is a warning only. An edge that isn't a whole unit snaps badly until you mark it with the **Edge** tool.

## Split a shape into several

**Multi-shape** splits the outline with lines. Use the **Line** tool to drag a line from edge to edge across the outline, and each closed area becomes a shape. **Vertex** moves the lines' ends as well as corners, and **Delete** removes a line. **Undo line** and **Clear lines** take lines back.

- **Save as rosette** makes a rosette of the parts. It needs two or more.
- **Save as shapes** adds each part as a shape of your own, leaving out parts that match shapes you already have.

Edges the lines make that aren't whole units are marked for you.

## Print a shape

The **Print** tab prints the shape as paper and fabric templates. Set the **1U length** and **How many rosettes** (here, how many pieces). The **3D Print** tab makes a cutting template to print on a 3D printer, with its **Size** in units. See [Printing](/help/guides/printing).
