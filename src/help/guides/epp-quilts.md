English paper piecing (EPP) has three kinds of item in RRQuilts. A **shape** is one template, such as a hexagon or a 60° diamond (see [EPP shapes](/help/guides/epp-shapes)). A **rosette** is a group of shapes sewn edge to edge, such as a hexagon flower (see [Rosettes](/help/guides/rosettes)). An **EPP quilt** is a layout of shapes and rosettes. The [traditional EPP demo](/?open_demo=traditional-epp.rrq) has a Grandmother's Flower Garden, a Lone Star and cube corners to look at and try. Objects sewn from flat pieces, such as balls and dice, are in [3D EPP](/help/guides/epp-3d).

## Units

Shapes and rosettes have no size of their own. Their edges are counted in units (U), so a hexagon at scale 1 has 1U edges. An EPP quilt sets how long 1U is with its **1U length**, and a printout of a shape or rosette sets its own. Pieces are placed at scale 1 to 4, so a hexagon at scale 2 has 2U edges.

An edge that isn't a whole number of units is **marked**. A marked edge snaps to edges of the same length, and to edges double, half or a quarter as long.

## The EPP tab

A project's **EPP** tab lists its own shapes and rosettes, then those of the libraries you use, grouped by library and category as blocks are. **All**, **Shapes** and **Rosettes** pick which ones show.

- **New shape** opens the shape editor on a 1U square.
- **New rosette** opens the rosette editor with nothing placed yet.
- **Import** brings in a shape or rosette saved as an `.rrqepp` file.

Each card's menu has **Open** and **Print**. Your own items also have **Duplicate**, **Export (.rrqepp)** to save one as a file to share, **Add to My library** and **Delete**. Library shapes and rosettes open read-only, and **Edit a copy** makes one of your own.

The built-in library has the regular polygons (triangle, square, pentagon, hexagon and octagon), irregular hexagons (half hexagon, honeycomb and elongated hexagon), the half square triangle, diamonds (60°, 45°, the Hexi Kite and the jewel) and a few rosettes.

## Start an EPP quilt

1. Choose **Start a quilt** on the home page, or **New quilt** in the project's **Quilts** tab, and pick **EPP**.
2. Set the **1U length** (2 in or 5 cm to start) and the project's **EPP seam allowance** (3/8 in or 10 mm). Both can change later.
3. **Borders** starts at none. Borders show once the quilt has a straight edge (see [Edge](#edge)).
4. Choose **Create quilt**. The quilt opens in its **Design** tab with nothing placed.

## The Design tab

The sidebar's **Shapes** tab picks what to place, and **Colors** paints. **Shapes and rosettes** lists this project's, then the libraries'. The **Scale** slider sets the size of what you place. A rosette with several colorings shows how many on its card. Tap the number to choose the coloring to place. **Place rosettes ungrouped** places a rosette as its loose pieces instead of one linked rosette.

**Placement** decides how pieces go down. With **Free** you place and drag pieces, and edges snap together. With **Grow** you tap a free edge to attach the picked shape to it. With **Grid** pieces sit on a grid, and a neighbor's edge in reach snaps first. In Grid, **Drag grid** moves the grid until it lines up with a placed piece, and **Sync grid to selected size** keeps the grid the size of the picked piece.

The tools on the left:

- **Select** picks pieces. Tap a piece to pick or unpick it, drag from empty space to box several (hold Alt to take every piece the box touches), and drag a picked piece to move them all. Turn, the Mirror tools, Delete, Break into patches, Duplicate and the Scale slider then act on what is picked.
- **Place** puts the picked shape or rosette where you tap. Tap its card again to stop. Drag a placed piece to move it.
- **Turn** turns a piece. A piece snapped to a neighbor turns to its next edge that fits the same neighbor. Otherwise it turns in steps that keep its edges on the directions its own edges and corners use, 60° for a hexagon and 30° for a Hexi Kite.
- **Mirror left to right** and **Mirror up and down** flip a piece where it is.
- **Break into patches** turns a placed rosette into loose pieces.
- **Delete** removes a piece.
- **Find** picks what a piece was placed from. Tap a piece and its shape or rosette is picked in the sidebar, at the piece's scale and, for a rosette, in the coloring it shows. Every piece placed from it glows. To change a placed rosette's coloring, pick another coloring on its card and place it over the old one.
- **Close space** fills a gap. Tap a gap to fill it with a shape, or drag from corner to corner to close part of a gap or to split a piece. A filled area that matches one of your shapes or a library's uses it. Any other becomes a shape of the quilt's own.
- **Paint** paints a piece with the picked color or fabric. **Apply to** paints **This** piece, **All** pieces, every piece of the **Same shape**, or every piece with the **Same fill**. Painting a piece of a placed rosette changes it in this quilt only.
- **Duplicate** (Ctrl+D) turns the picked pieces into a temporary rosette at the top of the sidebar, to place as often as you like until the next Duplicate.

Pieces that overlap show red. **Undo** and **Redo** are at the top of the tools. Colorways work as in other quilts (see [Designing a quilt](/help/guides/designing-a-quilt)).

## The Layout tab

**1U length** sets how long one unit is. **Seam allowance** is the project's EPP seam allowance, used by every EPP quilt and rosette in the project. **3D EPP** makes the quilt a design sewn into an object (see [3D EPP](/help/guides/epp-3d)).

## Edge

**Edge** decides how the quilt ends.

- **No trimming** follows the outer pieces. There are no borders or binding.
- **Cut to size** cuts a rectangle across the outer pieces after piecing, and the templates stay whole. With **Largest rectangle** it's the biggest rectangle the pieces fill with no gaps, and it changes as you add, move or remove pieces. With **Manual** you set it yourself. The picture shows the pieces it cuts away faded. Drag a side or a corner to resize it, or the round handle in the middle to move it, or type its **Width** and **Height** (typing a size switches to Manual). **Aspect ratio** keeps its shape, such as **Square (1:1)** or **Tall (3:4)**, and **Freeform** lets each side move on its own. **Snap to vertex** and **Snap to edge** make a dragged side stop at piece corners and along straight up-and-down or level piece edges near it.
- **Auto-close** fills the notches up to a straight edge with closing pieces, so nothing is cut. Pick a color or fabric in the Design tab, then choose **Use the picked color or fabric** for the closing pieces, or **None**.

## Borders

Borders go round a straight edge, so they show once **Edge** is **Cut to size** or **Auto-close**. The **Borders** tab then works as for any quilt.

## Printing

The **Print** tab has the quilt picture, a yardage estimate, and paper and fabric templates for the pieces, one for each shape and fill with how many to cut. Fabric templates use the project's EPP seam allowance. See [Printing](/help/guides/printing). The **3D Print** tab makes cutting templates to print on a 3D printer.
